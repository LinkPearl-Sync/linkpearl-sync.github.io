Linkpearl Sync

Your friends see your look.
No one else does.

Your mods go straight from your game to theirs, end-to-end encrypted: no server can read or keep them.

  • End-to-end encrypted
  • No files stored on a server
  • No account
  • Open source, self-hostable
What servers can and cannot see ›

  • Straight from game to game

    Your game talks directly to your friend's. When that's impossible, a relay passes along bytes it cannot read.

  • You choose who

    Right-click to ask, one click to accept. Nothing is shared unless you both agree.

  • Your whole look

    Penumbra and Glamourer, plus animations, VFX, sounds, Customize+, Heels, Honorific, Moodles.
